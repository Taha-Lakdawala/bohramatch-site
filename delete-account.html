Delete your BohraMatch account

Two ways to do it. No explanation needed, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

From inside the app

  1. Open BohraMatch and go to the My profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right corner.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you no longer have the app

Email bohramatch@gmail.com from any address, with the phone number your account uses in the message. We will verify that the number is yours before deleting anything, then confirm to you when it is done. Allow up to 7 days for us to reply, and no more than 30 days for the deletion itself.

What happens

Immediately: your profile stops appearing to anyone, your matches and conversations are closed, and you are signed out.

Within 30 days: everything below is permanently erased from our systems.

The one thing we keep

If someone reported your account for abuse, or you reported someone else, the report itself is kept after deletion. Otherwise anyone could erase a safety record simply by making a new account.

The report keeps what was reported and when. The identifying details of a deleted account are removed from it, and it is never used for anything except keeping members safe. If you have a specific concern about a report involving you, email us.

We may also keep records where the law requires us to. Backups are rotated on their own schedule and any copy of your data in a backup ages out with that backup.

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling BohraMatch from your phone does not delete your account. Use one of the two routes above.

1. Who we are

BohraMatch is a matrimony app for the Dawoodi Bohra community, operated from India. BohraMatch is the data controller for the data described here, and the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

Contact: bohramatch@gmail.com

2. You must be 18 or over

BohraMatch is for adults. Accounts are refused at signup if the date of birth given is under 18, and a government ID is checked against it during verification. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it and everything in it.

4. Religion, community and other sensitive data

BohraMatch exists to serve one religious community, so using it necessarily reveals something about your religious affiliation. The rida or topi photo is a picture of religious observance. Under the GDPR this is special category data (Article 9); under India's DPDP Act 2023 it is sensitive personal data.

We process it only on your explicit consent, which we ask for separately and in plain language at the point you submit that photo, and only for the single stated purpose of confirming community membership. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

We do not do biometric processing. The comparison between your selfie and your profile photos is made by a person looking at them. No face-recognition or face-matching software is used at any point, and no biometric template is created or stored.

5. Guardian details

If you choose to add a guardian's name and phone number, you are giving us someone else's personal data. Please only add it with their knowledge. It is never shown to other members, is used only if we need to contact your family about a safety matter, and is deleted with your account.

7. What other people can see

These rules are enforced on our server, not in the app, so they cannot be bypassed:

  • Your name. Someone browsing sees only your first initial. Your first name appears once you match, or earlier if you turn that on yourself.
  • Your date of birth never leaves our server. Only your age in years is sent to anyone else.
  • Blurred photos are not sent at all. If you choose to keep your photos hidden, other people's devices never receive the image, blurred or otherwise. Matching does not unhide them. Only you can reveal them, to one specific person at a time.
  • Incognito, if you turn it on, keeps your profile out of other people's daily decks.
  • Your phone number, email, exact date of birth, ITS number, guardian details and verification documents are never shown to another member.

Screen capture and screen recording are blocked inside the app. This is a real protection but not a perfect one, since nothing can stop someone photographing a screen with another device. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing.

9. Where your data is stored

Our server and database are hosted in Mumbai, India (Oracle Cloud ap-mumbai-1). If you use BohraMatch from outside India, your data is transferred to and stored in India. Where the GDPR applies to you, that transfer is made on the basis of your consent to use a service you know to be operated from India, and we use Standard Contractual Clauses with providers who require them.

10. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Login codes are one-time, short-lived and rate limited. There is no password to steal.
  • Session tokens are stored on our server only as irreversible hashes, and in your device's secure keystore on your side.
  • Photo metadata is stripped on upload. Photos from a phone often carry GPS coordinates in their EXIF data. We remove it before the file is written to disk, so the place you took the picture is never stored or published.
  • Verification documents are written to a separate location that is not connected to the public web at all. Profile photos and identity documents are never in the same place.
  • Your profile is frozen while it is under review, so nothing can be swapped in after a reviewer has looked at it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as quickly as we can, and in any case within the deadlines the law sets.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR (UK and EU), India's DPDP Act 2023 and the CCPA (California), you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your account and your data
  • Withdraw consent you have given, including for the community verification photo
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Ask for your data in a portable format
  • Complain to your data protection authority. In India, that is the Data Protection Board; in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your national supervisory authority.

Email bohramatch@gmail.com to exercise any of these. We will respond within 30 days. We will never charge you for a request or make you worse off for having made one.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or by following the instructions on this page. No explanation is required and nobody will try to talk you out of it. Your profile disappears from other people's view immediately, and everything listed in section 11 is removed on the schedule described there.

14. Children

BohraMatch is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, email us and we will remove it.

15. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address in order to serve the font file; nothing else about you is sent.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers. It talks only to our own server and to the push notification service named in section 8.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page. For a change that materially affects you, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect. Where the change requires your consent, we will ask for it rather than assume it.

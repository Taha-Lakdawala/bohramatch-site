Child safety standards

BohraMatch is an adults-only matrimony service. Child sexual abuse and exploitation has no place on it, and this page sets out the standards we hold ourselves to.

Last updated 12 September 2026

Adults only, and it is enforced

You must be 18 or older to hold a BohraMatch account. This is not only a line on the signup screen: a date of birth under 18 is refused by the database itself, so an underage profile cannot be created by any route into the service, including our own tools.

  • Every profile is reviewed by a person before it becomes visible to anyone else.
  • Identity is verified by a person, not an algorithm: a government-issued ID and a live selfie are compared by a reviewer.
  • Verification images are never given a public address. They are visible only to a reviewer, through an authenticated endpoint, and never to another user.

What is prohibited

We have zero tolerance for any of the following, and there is no warning step:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, shared anywhere in the app.
  • Sexualising a minor in a photo, in profile text, or in a message.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to make contact with a minor.
  • Holding, or trying to create, an account for anyone under 18.
  • Trafficking, or advertising a minor for any purpose.
  • Offering, requesting or linking to any of the above, on or off the app.

This applies to content sent privately between matched users exactly as it applies to a public profile. A private conversation is not a place where these rules stop.

How to report a concern

Reporting is built into the app and takes two taps. You do not need a match, a conversation, or a reason typed out.

  • On any profile — open it and tap Report or block.
  • In any conversation — tap the menu in the top right and choose to report or block.

Reporting blocks that person immediately and sends the report to a moderator. A report is kept even if the account that filed it, or the account it was filed against, is later deleted — deletion does not erase a safety record.

You can also write to bohramatch@gmail.com at any time, including if you do not have the app. Mark it urgent and it is treated as such.

What happens to a report

  • A person reviews it. Reports that allege child sexual abuse or exploitation are taken first, ahead of everything else in the queue.
  • Where the allegation is substantiated, the account is terminated permanently, the content is removed, and the person is not permitted to return.
  • We preserve the relevant evidence rather than deleting it, so that it is available to law enforcement.
  • We report to the relevant national and regional authorities. BohraMatch is operated from India, where reports are made through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and we comply with the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement requests relating to child safety.

Point of contact

bohramatch@gmail.com reaches the person responsible for child safety compliance at BohraMatch, including for law enforcement, regulators and app store review.

Why this page exists

These standards are published to meet Google Play's child safety standards policy and the equivalent requirement on the App Store, and because a service that asks people to verify who they are owes them a clear answer about how it keeps children out. They sit alongside our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

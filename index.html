Matrimony for the Dawoodi Bohra community, without the noise.

A small, deliberately slow app. Fifteen profiles a day, every one of them a real person who has been verified by hand.

Status

BohraMatch is in private beta and is not yet available in the app stores. This site exists so the privacy policy, terms and account-deletion route are published before the first person signs up.

Everyone is verified. Nobody skips it.

A profile cannot appear to anyone until all three checks below have been submitted and reviewed together, by a person, as one decision.

Check one

Government ID

Proves the person is real and over 18.

Check two

Photo in rida or topi

Proves they are part of the community this app is for.

Check three

Live selfie

Proves the account holder is the person in the photos.

The documents are deleted within 24 hours of review. We keep the verdict, never the ID. A reviewer compares the selfie to the profile photos by eye; there is no face-matching software anywhere in the product.

What we chose not to do

  • No infinite scroll. Fifteen profiles a day, then the deck is done.
  • No paid tiers, no boosts, no purchases of any kind.
  • No advertising, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • No screenshots. Screen capture is blocked inside the app.
  • No exact birth date leaves our server. Other people see an age, nothing more.
  • Strangers see an initial, not your name, until you match or choose otherwise.
